title: Conventions
description: Learn about Jumpedia's conventions.
---

Conventions are the general rules and recommendations that Jumpedia follows when defining and handling its data.

These conventions help keep information consistent across communities and make Jumpedia easier to understand and use. They cover things such as how strings are compared, how names and aliases work, and how names should generally be written.
