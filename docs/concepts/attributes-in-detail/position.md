title: Position
description: Learn how attributes are ordered when displayed.

The position controls the display order of attributes.

When attributes and their columns are displayed, they are sorted by their position. Attributes with a lower position appear before attributes with a higher position.

This allows communities to control the order of attributes without changing their names.

Positions start at 1 and count upwards until **n**, which is the number of attributes in the given list.
