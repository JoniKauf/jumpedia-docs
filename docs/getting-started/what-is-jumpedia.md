Jumpedia is a Discord bot that helps communities organize, manage, and track [tasks](/concepts/tasks-and-attributes-overview).

"Task" is the abstract term for any completable objective, either in real life or online. Examples include books, movies, speedruns, trickjumps, challenge runs, games, or anything else a community wants to track.

Members of a community can [give](/commands/basic/task#task-give) themselves tasks to mark them as completed. They can also provide additional information, such as proof or other user-specific data.

Community staff can manage and organize tasks in many different ways using fully customizable attributes, allowing each community to define its own structure and workflow.

All Jumpedia data is stored in a shared database, meaning it is accessible across all servers linked to a community.

With Jumpedia, communities can:

- Create and manage lists containing tasks.
- Define custom attributes that describe specific data points about their tasks.
- Assign tasks to users and track completion progress.
- Use commands and simple DSLs to efficiently interact with all the data.

Jumpedia is designed to adapt to many different types of communities while providing a consistent and powerful way to store, browse, and manage information.
